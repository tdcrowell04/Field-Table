# Field Table: verbs

*Written Oct 26, 2026. A proposal for Triston's accept or veto (ledger round 6). The 25 slice verbs are already ruled.*

Triston: *"Let's make sure that we have the requisite amount of verbs to cover almost any action that would be required in a tactical scenario and in a tabletop rpg scenario. You can also look at the actual Field Table artifact and see what verbs have been used in the games I've played."*

## How verbs work

- Every order becomes one verb with blanks: **who**, **what or where**, **how**, and **when** (a trigger like "once Bravo reports in position").
- **How** blanks change the way a verb is done without needing a new verb: speed (walk, run, crawl), quiet or not, posture, distance to keep ("maintaining distance from enemy pack"), which way to face ("oriented East"), and when to open fire ("open fire according to SOP").
- A verb is a recipe of pieces the game already has (move, play an animation, roll a die, apply an effect). That's also how modders make new ones.
- Anything no verb covers goes to the AI to decide, as ruled.

## Key

- ★ In the squad slice (the 25, already ruled)
- ● You used it in your Field Table games. The quote is your own order.
